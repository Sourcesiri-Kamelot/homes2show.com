### 📝 **NAR SETTLEMENT COMPLIANCE STRATEGY**

#### **Buyer Agreement Policy:**
- **NO requirement for Buyer Agreements to schedule showings**
- **"We do not enforce any regulation or agreement"**
- **Agents handle their own client relationships and documentation**

#### **Allowed Scenarios (No Buyer Agreement Required):**
1. **Listing agent shows to unrepresented buyer** (NAR Article 61)
2. **Open Houses** - Public events
3. **Rental Showings** - Property management initiated
4. **Inspector/Appraiser Access** - Service provider access
5. **Non-NAR agents** - In states without buyer agreement requirements

#### **Agent Delegation Scenarios:**
- **NAR Members with Buyer Agreements** can delegate showings (NAR Article 62)
- **Showing delegation clauses** in buyer agreements recommended
- **Agent cooperation** expected for state law compliance

- **Pay-per-showing model** - Initiating agents pay for each showing
- **24-hour payment delay** - Charged after showing completion
- **Cancellation policy:**

  - 30 minutes to 2 hours before: $20 partial charge
  - Within 30 minutes: Full charge

#### **Agent Relationships:**
- **Showing agents are independent contractors**
- **No commission sharing** - Showing agents paid per service
- **Initiating agents keep full commission**
- **Platform facilitates, doesn't supervise**
